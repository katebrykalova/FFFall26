The Problem

Gift giving is a recurring source of stress. Most people don't think about gifts year-round — the search only begins once a birthday, holiday, or anniversary is already approaching. This leads to last-minute brainstorming, endless scrolling through "top gift" lists, and settling for options that are either out of stock, impossible to personalize in time, or simply not very thoughtful.

Meanwhile, the best gift ideas are usually missed entirely — a passing comment, a small hint, an item noticed once and quickly forgotten. Without a system to capture these details when they happen, that information is lost by the time gift season actually arrives.

The Solution

Gifted is an app that runs quietly in the background, all year long, functioning as a personal gift-idea archive for the people in your life. Instead of starting from scratch every time an occasion approaches, users build simple ongoing profiles for the people they gift regularly.

How It Works

  1. Create a profile for each person you regularly gift, including interests, sizes, and things they've mentioned wanting.
  2. Add to the profile any time something comes up — a comment in conversation, an item noticed in passing, or an idea that crosses your mind.
  3. Gifted's built-in AI continuously scans for new products that match each person's profile, building a running list of personalized gift ideas automatically.
  4. When a gift needs extra lead time — like a custom order or personalization — Gifted sends an advance notification, so the order can be placed with enough time to arrive.

Key Features

  • Year-round profile builder for every person you gift
  • A single place to capture gift ideas as they come up, instead of scattered notes
  • AI-powered, continuously updated gift suggestions personalized to each profile
  • Advance alerts for custom or personalized orders that require extra lead time

Why It Matters

Gifted turns gift-giving from a stressful, last-minute scramble into something thoughtful and effortless. By capturing details the moment they happen and surfacing them exactly when needed, it helps people give gifts that genuinely show they were paying attention — without the mental load of trying to remember everything on their own.
