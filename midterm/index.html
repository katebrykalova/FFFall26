About PathFinder

The problem: Most computer science students know they want to work in tech, but there are so many career paths that it's hard to know which one actually fits. Job titles sound alike, and it's not obvious what each role does day to day.

The solution: PathFinder breaks down the most common CS careers in plain language, then matches you to a path based on what you enjoy most, so you can focus your classes, projects, and internships with confidence.
